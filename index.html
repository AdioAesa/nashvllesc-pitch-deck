How It Works: 3-Step System

Win Contribution (PAR)

Points Above Replacement

  • • Estimates league points contributed
  • • Position-specific weighting
  • • Scale: -10 (replacement) to +20 (elite)
  • • 1 goal differential ≈ 0.662 points
Every player on one currency — win contribution vs. a replacement — so a Brazilian winger and a Danish defender compare directly

Total Cost (TCO)

Beyond Transfer Fees

  • • Transfer Fee + (Wages × Contract)
  • • Typical: 26% wages, 74% transfer
  • • Age-adjusted compensation
  • • League-specific multipliers
South American Prospect: $1.5M transfer + $800K wages = $2.3M TCO

Injury Risk & Availability

Availability-Adjusted Metrics

  • • Tracks injury history
  • • Adjusts PAR for games available
  • • Risk categories: Very Low → High
  • • True cost per available win
95% available vs 60% available = 58% more value!
